03 / THE ESSENTIALS

What the ratio can — and can't — tell you.

The length-to-width ratio describes a diamond's face-up proportions. It is a useful way to discuss your preferred shape and compare outlines from grading reports.

It does not measure sparkle, symmetry, cut craftsmanship, certification, colour or clarity. Two diamonds with the same ratio may look different because of their facet patterns and other characteristics.

Frequently asked questions

How is the ratio calculated?

Divide the face-up length by the width, using the same units for both. For example, 9.00 mm ÷ 6.00 mm = 1.50, written as 1.50:1.

Is there one ideal ratio for each shape?

No universal ratio suits every person or stone. Outline preference varies, and a ratio alone cannot determine beauty or optical quality.

Does this work for lab-grown diamonds?

Yes. The calculation depends on measurements and applies equally to natural and laboratory-grown diamonds.

Are my measurements stored?

No. Calculations run in your browser. The tool does not send measurement data to a server. A share link includes the measurements in its URL only if you choose to copy it.
